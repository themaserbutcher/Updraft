      Right thumb
      A real stick. Pull back (drag down) to raise the nose, push forward to lower it, sideways to bank.
      Tilt to fly
      Or steer by tilting the phone: turn it like a wheel to bank, tilt the top towards you to pull up. Switch it on here, with the phone button at the top, or in the menu.
      Rudder bar
      Slide to yaw. Use it with the stick to keep the yaw string straight.
      Brakes
      Drag down to open the airbrakes and steepen your approach.
      Drag the view
      Orbit the camera around the glider in the chase views, or look around from the cockpit. The recentre button snaps it back.
      Radio
      Tap the radio button at the top to change station. The music follows your flight: it builds as you climb, tightens near a stall, and a guitar kicks in above 100 kt. Music volume, and a steady option, are in the menu.
      Map
      Turns with you, so ahead is up; N marks north. Tap it to see all the islands.
      Aircraft
      Beside the glider on this screen: up and down pick the aircraft, left and right its paint. Swipe the glider to do the same.
      Throttle
      Piper Cub only: slide the lever beside the brakes up for power.
      Full screen
      The corners button at the top. On an iPhone, add Updraft to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and open it from there: it runs full screen.
